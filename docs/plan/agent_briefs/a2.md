# Task A2: works on any device without the user having to configure anything

Owner (05.11.2026): «Проверяем функционал почты, календаря, контактов, уведомлений, перевода, работы в фоне и push … для популярных прошивок и форков, с сервисами Google и без них (доступ к данным и разрешения, заблокированный экран, уведомления, создание и редактирование контактов, работа в фоне). Это должно быть полностью работоспособное приложение на любых устройствах с учётом требуемых разрешений. Пользователь не должен думать, что ему включить и настроить.» Rules: [r2_common.md](r2_common.md) (strings → `tool/l10n/pending/a2.json`).

## Scope
An end-to-end audit with fixes, Android first, then iPhone/iPad:
- **Devices:** stock Android/Pixel, Samsung One UI, Xiaomi MIUI/HyperOS, Huawei EMUI/HarmonyOS (no Google services), Honor MagicOS, OPPO ColorOS / OnePlus OxygenOS / realme UI, vivo OriginOS/Funtouch, nubia/ZTE MyOS (the owner's phone, Android 15), Motorola; LineageOS, GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS — with microG and without any Google services. Android 8–16 (targetSdk as in the project).
- **Areas:** mail sync and new-mail notifications (WorkManager, foreground sync service, server signals: FCM when Google Play services exist, UnifiedPush otherwise, periodic checks as the last resort); calendar and contacts (device calendar/contacts plugins, phone account/mirror — read, create, edit); notifications (Android 13 POST_NOTIFICATIONS, channels, lock screen visibility and privacy — content hidden on the lock screen when the user/organisation wants it, MDM rule `notificationsNoContent`), exact alarms for reminders (Android 12–14 SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM rules and Play policy), boot/restore after reboot and app update, battery optimisation and OEM autostart/background restrictions, translation (ML Kit needs Google Play services? — only report; agent T2 owns translation code), sign-in through the browser (AppAuth / custom tabs without Chrome).
- **iPhone/iPad:** notification permission flow, APNs relay signals, background refresh (BGTask), calendar/contacts permissions and Info.plist usage strings, lock-screen notification privacy.

## What "the user does not have to think" means here
- Every permission is asked by the app at the moment it is needed, with one short explanation, and never more than needed (no READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE/MEDIA permissions if the system photo picker suffices — check; remove what is unused).
- After the first mailbox is added, the app checks what this device needs for reliable new-mail notifications (notification permission, battery optimisation exemption, OEM autostart/background screen, a signal channel: FCM / UnifiedPush / periodic) and offers ONE guided step with buttons that open the exact system screen for this ROM (existing code: `platform_diagnostics.dart`, `background_hint_strip.dart`, `permissions_settings_screen.dart`, `BackgroundDiagnostics.kt`). Nothing appears when everything is already fine.
- Without Google services: no errors, no dead buttons — FCM silently absent, UnifiedPush offered when a distributor is installed, otherwise periodic checks with the right expectations; translation hidden or explained.
- After reboot/update: background work and alarms come back by themselves.
